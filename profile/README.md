# Thicker Than Mud

A kavatender's guide to organizing your bar, and the shared resources that go with it.

Solidarity is thicker than mud.

## What we do

We make a printable zine for kava workers, and we share what we learn organizing bars across South Florida and beyond. The zine is free. Copy it, fold it, pass it on.

## Our repos

- **thicker-than-mud** — the zine archive. Issues 01 and 02, in narrow quarter size for a normal stapler, and in pocket eighth size.
- **kava-workers-resources** — log templates, contacts, and short guides for organizing your bar.

## The zine

Issue 01, Winter 2026: your rights, the steps from shell to contract, the boss's playbook, and who's got your back.

Issue 02, Spring 2027: the work behind the mud. Wage theft in detail, scheduling, closing alone, bodies, surveillance and discipline, where the money goes, and the general union explained.

## Print it

The narrow quarter version is the one to print first. It is 3.5 by 5.5 inches, it fits a normal stapler, and each issue has a reading PDF and a folded print PDF. Full print instructions are in the README of the thicker-than-mud repo.

## Safety

Do not post personal information about workers or bars here without their explicit say so. No names, no phone numbers, no screenshots with group chat names. If you are organizing, talk to an organizer first: EWOC, workerorganizing.org.
